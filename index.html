Before You Start

When listing a book, step 1 uses Gemini Vision to read the title and ISBN from the cover. Step 2 prompts you to snap 1–2 photos of random inside pages. The AI analyzes those photos for pen/pencil marks, torn edges, or yellow highlighting, automatically classifying the book's condition badge (*Like New*, *Good*, or *Annotated*).

During signup, students under 18 enter their parent or guardian's email. An automated verification link and 6-digit OTP are sent to the guardian. Once confirmed, full community interaction features are unlocked while maintaining Guardian AI chat monitoring.
To protect minor accounts and prevent fraud, we log encrypted User IDs, verified `.edu` emails, login timestamps (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`), and 3-strike moderation events. Raw password strings and continuous GPS data are never stored.
