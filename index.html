Before you start.

Is my home address or live GPS location shared?

Never. Shiori GLOBAL never stores continuous GPS or home addresses. All swaps happen via Safe-Exchange Pins inside school campuses or public libraries.

How are students verified?

Students verify using their school/college ID or institutional email. Once verified, raw ID photos are immediately deleted and only a verified status badge is kept.

Can students under 18 use Shiori GLOBAL?

Yes! Accounts for students under 18 include a Guardian Consent verification flow and strict Shiori Guardian AI moderation to keep the community 100% safe.
