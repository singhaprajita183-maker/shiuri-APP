Before you start.

How does the AI know if a book has pen marks inside? +

Our scanner uses a 2-step flow: Step 1 scans the cover/ISBN to write the book description, and Step 2 prompts the seller to snap 1–2 inside pages so Gemini Vision can check for highlighting, pen notes, or wear before listing.

How do payments and escrow work when buying or selling? +

Free swaps and donations have zero fees. For paid Level 3+ sales, buyer payments are held securely in escrow until book handover is confirmed—then released to the seller minus a 5%–8% platform fee. Shiori Pro subscriptions go directly toward funding platform AI & operations.

How is student privacy protected for under-18 users? +

Under-18 accounts require automated parent/guardian email approval, home addresses are never stored, chats are guarded 24/7 by our 3-strike Guardian AI, and logins are protected with 60-second expiring email OTPs.
