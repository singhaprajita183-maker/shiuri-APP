How the estimates work

For demonstration only, this prototype uses 0.5 kg CO₂e per reused book and 0.01 tree-equivalent per book. These are configurable illustrative assumptions, not verified lifecycle measurements or official ESG figures.

Build a trustworthy impact system

A production version should document its methodology, distinguish estimated from verified impact, prevent duplicate claims and retain auditable records of completed exchanges.
